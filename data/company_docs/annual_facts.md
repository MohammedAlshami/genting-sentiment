## Notes and caveats

1. Units: GENT and GENM report in RM million. Earlier quarterly reports (through 4Q25)
   were in RM'000; the parsed quarterly dataset was normalised to RM million.
2. "Adjusted EBITDA" is the companies' own non-IFRS measure, taken from their results
   press releases; it excludes non-recurring items and is not PBT + finance cost + D&A.
3. Total debt here means long-term + short-term borrowings, **excluding lease liabilities**
   (GENT lease liabilities were RM1,517.1m at end-2025; GENM RM1,449.3m).
4. FY2024 figures are the comparative column in the FY2025 annual reports and agree with
   the FY2024 annual reports to the stated rounding.
5. The FY2025 annual reports were published in 2026 and are the latest available at the
   collection date (2 Nov 2026).
