## RAM Ratings — 12 Dec 2025

**Headline:** Outlook on Genting Group revised to negative; long-term corporate
credit ratings maintained at **AA1**.

Full public text: `RAM_2026_outlook_negative.txt` (and `.html`).
Source: <https://www.ram.com.my/pressrelease?prviewid=7152>

Key points from the public release:

- Outlook revised to negative from stable for Genting Berhad and Genting Malaysia Berhad;
  AA1 long-term ratings and issue ratings maintained.
- Drivers: completion of the take-over offer for GENM raised the stake to 73%, and
  capex for the full casino development at Resorts World New York City.
- RAM estimated capex/investment could exceed RM8 billion through FY2027; net gearing
  could reach ~0.6x and net debt/OPBDIT ~3.4x in FY2026; FFO net-debt cover could
  fall below 0.3x.

Source: <https://www.fitchratings.com/research/corporate-finance/fitch-downgrades-genting-berhad-to-bbb-resorts-world-las-vegas-to-bb-outlooks-stable-07-09-2026>

The article body is JavaScript/paywalled; `Fitch_2026-09-07_downgrade_BBB-.html`
retains the public page shell. Publicly indexed summary points captured with the page:

- Consolidated cash and cash equivalents around MYR19 billion at end-2025 and over
  MYR18 billion at end-June 2026.
- Consolidated debt maturities of around MYR19 billion over 2026–2028.
- Fitch forecast negative free cash flow averaging about MYR4 billion a year over
  2026–2028, with average annual capex of about MYR9.2 billion.
- Average annual dividend outflow (excluding minorities) of about MYR270 million
  over 2026–2028.
