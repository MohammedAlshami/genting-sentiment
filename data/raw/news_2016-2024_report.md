## Method

- Discovery: Google News RSS (`hl=en-MY&gl=MY&ceid=MY:en`) using entity, finance and outlet-scoped queries, walked across 33 quarter windows; any slice hitting the ~90-item RSS cap was split recursively.
- Redirect resolution: Google News batchexecute decoder to canonical publisher URL.
- Full text: trafilatura, readability and BeautifulSoup fallbacks; The Star `?s=app`, NST AMP and NST embedded-body JSON; page meta description only as a last-resort truncated fallback.
- The Sun and The Rakyat Post were additionally queried through their public WordPress REST APIs. The Star archive was walked through its public Queryly search API (3,190 indexed matches), which yielded 2020-2024 items that Google News had ranked below the cut.
- Dedupe: canonical URL, then exact body hash, then title similarity ≥0.90.
- Politeness: ≥1.15 s per host for article fetches, 1.2 s for Google News RSS, 0.4 s for redirect resolution, exponential backoff on 429/5xx.

- Months with no recovered articles (17): 2016-12, 2017-01, 2017-02, 2017-07, 2017-09, 2017-12, 2018-03, 2018-04, 2018-06, 2018-08, 2018-09, 2018-10, 2019-12, 2021-07, 2021-12, 2023-01, 2023-12.
- Thin months with 1–2 articles (18): 2016-11 (1), 2017-03 (2), 2017-06 (1), 2017-10 (2), 2018-02 (2), 2018-05 (2), 2018-07 (1), 2019-02 (1), 2019-05 (2), 2019-06 (2), 2019-09 (2), 2019-11 (1), 2020-01 (2), 2020-10 (1), 2021-09 (2), 2022-10 (1), 2023-03 (2), 2024-01 (2).
- Google News RSS indexes only what it has crawled. 2016–2019 coverage is much thinner than 2020–2024, so the total is a floor rather than a census.
- Bernama serves only a subscription notice on every article page, and BusinessToday / EdgeProp returned HTTP 403 to the article fetcher; those pages were dropped rather than storing non-article text, and are counted in the fetch failure table. Nine of those entries are deliberate exclusions of KLSE Screener announcement/utility pages rather than retrieval failures. No record in this file needed a `raw.truncated=true` flag because no partial body was retained without a usable article lead.
- Genting Singapore, Genting Plantations, Genting Hong Kong/cruise and other separately listed Genting entities are excluded unless the story is explicitly about Genting Berhad or Genting Malaysia.
- Place/lifestyle/concert copy set in Genting Highlands is excluded. KLSE Screener bursa announcements are excluded; its news pages are retained when they carry an article body.
- Dates come from publisher metadata where present, otherwise from the Google News `pubDate` (which is often date-only, normalised to 08:00 UTC).
