## Text and table extraction

- Per-report text: `data/company_docs/quarterly/text/<TICKER>_<YEAR>Q<Q>.txt`, with `===== PAGE n =====` markers.
- Per-report tables: `data/company_docs/quarterly/tables/<TICKER>_<YEAR>Q<Q>.csv`, long form with page, table index, row, column, value, row label, column label and strategy.
- Borderless Bursa tables are reconstructed from pdfplumber word coordinates; ruled tables use pdfplumber's default line strategy. No cell is dropped: 136,415 numeric cells were retained.
- Unresolved extraction issues (36) are logged in `data/raw/quarterly_extraction_issues.csv`; almost all are image-only PDFs or pre-2020 labels that do not affect the 2020–2026 panel.
- `data/raw/quarterly_numbers.csv` is the cross-report long table (company, ticker, year, quarter, page, table_index, row, col, value_text, value_number, row_label, col_label, context, strategy).

## Financial panel

`data/raw/quarterly_financials.csv` has **157 company-quarter rows** (84 GENTING, 73 GENM). All monetary values are **RM million**; EPS is **sen**; NAPS is **RM**.

Headline fields come from the condensed income statement and balance sheet. Segment revenue is taken from the geographic segment note (cumulative year-to-date); quarterly segment figures are derived by subtracting the prior quarter's cumulative figure. `segment_revenue_others_q` is the residual after Malaysia, Singapore, UK/Egypt and US/Bahamas. Every row cites the source PDF and page numbers in `source` and `pages`.

EBITDA is the company-reported **adjusted EBITDA** where the report provides a current-quarter figure; otherwise it is computed as `PBT + finance cost + quarterly D&A` from the cash-flow statement. `ebitda_basis` states which method was used.

## Known gaps and skips

- **GENTING 2013 Q4**: the IR page row exists but its PDF link is empty (`#`); no mirror copy was found. This is the only missing quarterly slot.
- **GENTING 2023 Q1** is image-only, so the 2020–2026 panel has 51 company-quarter rows (25 GENTING + 26 GENM) rather than 52.
- **Image-only reports**: 14 PDFs (13 GENM 2009–2013, GENTING 2023 Q1) have no text layer. They are downloaded and hashed but cannot contribute text/tables without OCR.
- **2026 Q3**: not yet announced as at 2 Nov 2026, so the latest quarter in the corpus is 2026 Q2.
- **Rights issue circular/prospectus**: not published by 2 Nov 2026. The 9 Oct 2026 announcement and its 22-page attachment are included; completion was targeted for 1Q2027.
- **Credit ratings**: RAM's press release is public and captured in full; Fitch and S&P article bodies are JavaScript/registration-walled, so only the public page shell and headline are stored.
- **Announcements, first weeks of the window**: no filing dated 2–24 Nov 2024 was found on either mirror; the earliest record in the window is 25 Nov 2024 (GENM material litigation). Both counters were quiet before the 3Q24 results on 28 Nov 2024.
- **Announcements**: Bursa's main site blocks plain requests; the disclosure iframe is used instead. Routine shareholding notices are taken from the KLSE Screener mirror, which carries the full body; Bursa is opened only for categories that can carry a PDF attachment.
- **i3investor**: its announcement page exposes only the latest ~100 records (from March 2025), so it cannot cover Nov 2024–Feb 2025; KLSE Screener/Bursa is the canonical enumerator.
