- Postings rarely state sponsorship; raise Global Talent Stream ($1k / 10 business days) in the first recruiter call.

## How #11–#27 were found and what the visa column can and can't tell you

- **Method (29 Sep 2026):** scanned ~15,900 company job boards (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever) → 442 Canada-based postings that mention LLM/agent work → kept only those with **posted pay ≥100K**, **≤4 yrs required** (no unstated-experience "Senior" titles), agent/LLM engineering work, one role per company, no repeats of #1–#10.
- **Why not 200:** only **~124 postings / 75 companies** passed pay + experience, and after removing non-agentic roles (security, data-science, GTM, etc.) about **17** were left. Padding to 200 would have meant senior roles or roles with no posted pay, so I stopped at what's real.
- **Visa reality:** across every posting scanned, **only Harvey (#3) explicitly offers relocation assistance**; zero postings promise visa sponsorship. Several US/Canada companies (e.g. Firecrawl, Lavendo) explicitly refuse sponsorship and were excluded.
- **Practical proxy for #14–#27:** hybrid/on-site rows (Socure, Homebase, Hinge Health, 7shifts, CookUnity, Kaseya, Diligent, Durable, Gumloop, Lyft) are the ones where an employer can realistically use Global Talent Stream. **Remote-only rows** (NerdWallet, Observe.AI, Confluent, WorkOS) almost always need existing Canadian work authorization.
- **Freshness caveat:** Durable's and WorkOS's postings were last updated Mar/Feb 2026 but are still on their boards; CookUnity's since Jul 2026. Confirm before investing time.
