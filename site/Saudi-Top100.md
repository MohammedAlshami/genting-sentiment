## Salary benchmarks — does SAR 15k/month hold at ~3 YOE?

Posted-pay evidence is thin: almost no Saudi posting shows a band. The two explicit local-agency bands undercut the floor — Techgropse Node.js 3–6 yrs in Jeddah at SAR 7,000–8,000/mo, and CleanLife's 3+ yr Full Stack band tops out at SAR 15,962/mo (ceiling touches the floor, floor is SAR 2,790). The three posted bands that clear it are Silver Edge (SAR 20.8–37.5k/mo), Entoss OpenShift (SAR 18–20k/mo, via Mustakbil), and Crossover remote (up to USD 200k/yr funnel cap; analyst seat at USD 60k/yr ≈ SAR 18.75k/mo).

Benchmarks split into two tiers. At or above the floor: SalaryExpert/ERI puts Riyadh AI Engineer entry (1–3 yrs) at ≈ SAR 14,700/mo and average at ≈ SAR 20,900/mo; Riyadh Software Engineer entry at ≈ SAR 15,300/mo; Edoxi puts mid (2–5 yrs) at SAR 15–25k/mo against entry 10–15k; Levels.fyi Riyadh median is ≈ SAR 17k/mo (n=62, MNC-skewed, Oct 2026); Hays KSA 2025 guide floors backend/full-stack at SAR 20,000–35,000/mo (Hays-placed, mostly senior/prime-employer roles); Cooper Fitch startup bands sit at SAR 26,000–36,000/mo; Aramco's median package is ≈ SAR 32k/mo (n=23). Below the floor: Glassdoor Riyadh SWE median is SAR 13k/mo (range 8–18k); PayScale Riyadh SWE averages ≈ SAR 7.9k/mo (n=96, SME-skewed — 15k is ~90th percentile there); Naukrigulf self-reported averages sit at SAR 7.4–7.8k/mo for 3–6 YOE; GulfTalent senior SE averages SAR 12.5k/mo (Riyadh subset 8k).

Reading: SAR 15k/mo is defensible but selective. It sits just above the Glassdoor median, just below the Levels.fyi (MNC-skewed) median, at the Edoxi entry-ceiling/mid-floor boundary, and well below Hays/Cooper Fitch prime-employer floors. In practice it targets the prime tier — MNCs, banks/fintech, Aramco/PIF entities, funded startups — and local dev shops posting SAR 7–8k will push back. No bank-specific (SNB, Al Rajhi) or NEOM/PIF-entity software bands were found; that part of the ≥15k case rests on Hays/vendor proxies, not postings.
